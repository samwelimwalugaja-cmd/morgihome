KAZI ZA PHASE HII (TEKELEZA MOJA KWA MOJA)
================================================================================

--- KAZI 2.1: Pakua Template ---
TEKELEZA:
1. Nenda kwenye: https://bootstrapmade.com/demo/Resi/
2. Pakua template (bonyeza "Download").
3. Fungua faili iliyopakuliwa (ZIP).
4. Nakili yaliyomo kwenye folder ya: backend/static/

--- KAZI 2.2: Unda Folder za Static na Templates ---
TEKELEZA:
1. Nenda kwenye backend/static/
2. Hakikisha una folders: css/, js/, images/, vendors/
3. Unda folder: backend/templates/
4. Unda faili: backend/templates/index.html

--- KAZI 2.3: Badilisha Content ya Landing Page ---
TEKELEZA:
1. Fungua backend/templates/index.html
2. Badilisha maandishi yafuatayo:
   - Jina la kampuni: "MorgiHome"
   - Hero Section: "Kupata Nyumba na Mikopo kwa Urahisi"
   - Subtitle: "Platform ya kisasa ya kusimamia mikopo ya nyumba na mali isiyohamishika"
3. Badilisha Navigation:
   - Home | Properties | Mortgages | About | Contact | Login/Signup

--- KAZI 2.4: Badilisha Rangi ---
TEKELEZA:
1. Fungua backend/static/css/style.css
2. Badilisha rangi kuwa za MorgiHome:
   - Primary Color: #0077B6 (Blue Bahari)
   - Secondary Color: #0A2B4E (Dark Blue)
   - Background: #FFFFFF (White)
3. Hakikisha buttons zote ni za Blue Bahari
4. Hakikisha header na footer ni Dark Blue

--- KAZI 2.5: Unganisha na Django ---
TEKELEZA:
1. Nenda kwenye morgihome_backend/settings.py
2. Hakikisha TEMPLATES ina:
   TEMPLATES = [
       {
           'BACKEND': 'django.template.backends.django.DjangoTemplates',
           'DIRS': [BASE_DIR / 'templates'],  # Hii ni muhimu!
           'APP_DIRS': True,
           ...
       },
   ]
3. Hakikisha STATICFILES_DIRS iko:
   STATICFILES_DIRS = [BASE_DIR / 'static']

--- KAZI 2.6: Unda URLs kwa Landing Page ---
TEKELEZA:
1. Nenda kwenye morgihome_backend/urls.py
2. Weka:
   from django.contrib import admin
   from django.urls import path, include
   from django.views.generic import TemplateView

   urlpatterns = [
       path('admin/', admin.site.urls),
       path('', TemplateView.as_view(template_name='index.html'), name='home'),
       path('api/auth/', include('accounts.urls')),
   ]

--- KAZI 2.7: Test Website ---
TEKELEZA:
1. Endesha server: python manage.py runserver
2. Fungua browser: http://127.0.0.1:8000/
3. Hakikisha inaonekana vizuri (landing page inaonekana)

--- KAZI 2.8: Hakikisha Ina-responsive ---
TEKELEZA:
1. Punguza ukubwa wa browser kwa simu (mobile view)
2. Hakikisha inaonekana vizuri
3. Hakikisha menu inafanya kazi (hamburger menu)
