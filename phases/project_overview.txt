================================================================================
PROJECT: MorgiHome - MAELEZO YA JUMLA
================================================================================

Hii ni platform ya usimamizi wa mikopo ya nyumba (mortgage) na mali isiyohamishika (real estate).

TEKNOLOJIA:
- Backend: Django (Python)
- Web Frontend: HTML, CSS, Bootstrap (Template: https://bootstrapmade.com/demo/Resi/)
- Mobile App: Flutter (Template: https://doctor-nu-two.vercel.app/)
- Database: PostgreSQL (au SQLite kwa sasa)
- Authentication: JWT (djangorestframework-simplejwt)

WATUMIAJI (ROLES) - WATU 5:
1. Customer (Mteja/Mkopaji)
2. Seller (Muuza nyumba)
3. Bank (Benki)
4. Real Estate Company (Kampuni ya Mali Isiyohamishika)
5. Lawyer (Wakili)

RANGI ZA KAMPUNI:
- Blue Bahari (Primary): #0077B6
- Dark Blue (Secondary): #0A2B4E
- White (Background): #FFFFFF

LENGO: Kuunda mfumo salama, wa kitaalamu, na wa kisasa unaowaunganisha wateja, wauzaji, benki, kampuni za mali isiyohamishika, na wakili katika mchakato mmoja wa kidijitali.
