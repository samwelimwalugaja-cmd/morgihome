Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about mortgages, properties and MorgiHome

Apply for Mortgage

1. What documents do I need to apply for a mortgage?

Valid ID (NIDA/passport), proof of income (3 payslips or business statements), 3-month bank statements, employer letter, employment contract, and certified copy of title deed. Your bank will provide the full checklist after pre-approval.

2. How long does mortgage approval take?

Once documents are complete, banks typically decide within 5–14 business days. Valuation and legal review add 3–7 days. MorgiHome tracks each step so you see live status.

3. Does MorgiHome charge any fees?

Browsing, calculator and initial consultation are free. Bank processing fees (0–1.5%), valuation and legal fees are paid directly to the bank/lawyer and are shown before you apply.

4. Are the properties on MorgiHome verified?

Yes. Every listing is inspected by licensed real estate professionals and title is verified via e-Ardhi before publishing. Look for the “Verified” badge.

5. Can I sell my home through MorgiHome?

Yes. Register as Property Seller, create a listing with photos and documents, and our team will verify and publish it to thousands of buyers and mortgage applicants.

6. Is my data secure on MorgiHome?

Absolutely. We use Argon2 hashing, JWT, HTTPS/HSTS, and encrypted storage. Documents are stored securely and only shared with your chosen bank and lawyer.

7. What types of mortgages are available?

Purchase (buy house/apartment), Refinance/Equity Release, Semi-Finish (complete at lintel stage), and Construction (build in 5 stages). NMB example: 10M–700M, construction up to 200M, max 15 years at 17%.

8. Do I need a down payment?

Yes, typically 10% for purchase loans (bank finances up to 90%). Down payment can be higher to lower monthly instalment — use the calculator on the homepage.

9. Can I apply if I am self-employed?

Yes. Self-employed and business owners are welcome. Provide business registration (BRELA), 6–12 month statements, and proof of steady income. MorgiHome supports both salaried and business profiles.

10. What happens if I miss a payment?

Contact your bank immediately. Late fees may apply and it can affect your credit score. MorgiHome sends reminders via the Repayment Schedule and you can arrange a plan with the bank.

11. How do I check my application status?

Log in → My Applications → Track. You will see: Submitted → CRB Check → Valuation → Credit Assessment → Approval → Disbursement with dates.

12. Can I pay off my mortgage early?

Yes. Early repayment is allowed with no penalty at most partner banks (e.g., NMB). You save on interest — confirm with your bank for the final settlement amount.
