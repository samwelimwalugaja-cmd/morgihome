Toggle Dark/Light via header moon/sun button. Choice is saved in localStorage.theme and applied as class="dark" on <html>.

Frequently Asked Questions

How to approve a loan?

Open Applications → Detail → Review → select Approve, set amount, add reason, confirm.

Why logout not working?

Click Logout in sidebar → clears localStorage and redirects to /accounts/login/. If still logged in, clear browser storage manually.

Where are contracts?

Contracts at /bank/contracts/ — filter by Active/Pending Signature.
